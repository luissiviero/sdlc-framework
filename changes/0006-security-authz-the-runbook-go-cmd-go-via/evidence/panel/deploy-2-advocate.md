## Verdict
Keep the finding Important and open for the owner's approving review; do not let the panel downgrade or close it as "declared", and do not park on it.

## The case against
The easy answer is "declared in spec.md, decided by design panel 1, committed before the lock, owner unlocked tests: not a real finding". Each leg is weaker than it looks.
- "Before the lock" is the rule's own target, not an exemption: `plugin/review/findings.py framework_findings` says it exists "for the paths a hook cannot see, e.g. an edit made in CI or before the lock". Dismissing the finding because the edit preceded the lock reads the check backwards.
- The intent's constraint is literal: "no pre-existing test is edited to make the fix pass". plan.md's Risks line admits the fixture line exists because "without the fixture line of step 1 the fix breaks five of them". That is an edit under `tests/` made so the fix passes, whatever its size; the design panel's "test level" reading is an interpretation the owner never wrote into REVIEW.md.
- The five pre-existing Go tests were re-scoped through the fixture rather than their bodies: `test_go_by_a_person_runs_the_runbook_and_reruns_the_gate` (tests/test_detect_cli.py:790) still claims "a person" in its name and proves "the repository owner" only because `GITHUB_REPOSITORY_OWNER=the-owner` now makes that actor the owner. A later reader of the suite sees a claim the test no longer makes.
- The `sdlc:unlock-tests` label (`status.yaml: tests_unlocked_by`) covered fix round 2's assertion change in the new collaborator test after the lock; it is not a recorded waiver of the REVIEW.md rule for the fixture edit, and the design PR's merge accepted risk item `auth`, not this finding.
What I checked that holds: the diff to the pre-existing file is exactly the `monkeypatch` parameter, one `setenv` line, two new tests, one helper and one constant; no body or assertion of an existing test changes; `failing-test.log` 1a shows 96 passed with the line alone and 1b shows the three new tests failing before the fix; `is_owner_actor` is reached in `plugin/detect/cli.py` only from `_human_label_actor` (line 1190), and `GITHUB_REPOSITORY_OWNER` is read only by `plugin/state/unpark.py owner_login`, so the fixture line is inert for the dispatch and dismiss tests that share the fixture. Nothing is weakened; an authz narrowing cannot leave tests that encode the permissive behaviour untouched, and the alternatives edit six bodies.
So: not a park. But the finding is the owner's guardrail speaking, and its resolution is the owner's approving review at gate (deploy), not a panel line. I would accept the easy answer if the owner's review names this finding as accepted, and I would want a later non-fix change to rename or re-document the five Go tests so their claims match what they now prove.
