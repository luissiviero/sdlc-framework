# Decisions versus evidence: the 34 settled decisions audited against the notes, PROGRESS and the shipped changes (2026-10-20)

Report only. Nothing is reopened here: the owner reopens decisions. Each decision in `docs/decisions/` was asked one question: has a platform fact in `docs/notes/`, a live check recorded in `docs/PROGRESS.md` or its archive, or a change shipped since the decision was taken contradicted its premise, weakened its reason or made it moot? An amendment the owner already accepted (decisions 21 to 34 amend 2, 3, 5, 11, 14, 18, 20, 21, 22, 24 and 27) counts as the owner's answer to earlier evidence, not as new evidence against the amended decision; the verdict below judges the decision as it stands after its amendments.

Four verdicts: **holds**; **holds, reason changed** (the decision stands, but the reason that carries it today is not the one written); **weakened** (evidence against its premise or reason, cited by file and line); **moot** (nothing in the plugin rests on it any more). For each decision not in "holds", the last column gives two sentences: what the evidence shows, and what the owner would decide if the decision were taken today.

Evidence read: `docs/decisions/index.md` and all 34 files; `docs/notes/index.md` and NOTES §2, §9, §17, §41, §45 to §51; `docs/PROGRESS.md` (2026-10-20); `docs/ROADMAP.md` (R-rows and Done); `docs/reviews/2026-10-19-scan-incidents-55-57-triage.md`; `template/sdlc.yaml`; the sample's `sdlc.yaml`.
