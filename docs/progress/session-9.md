103. **The runner re-verifies what the session recorded** (the owner's decision, 2026-09-27, asked in this session's questions; not a new decision in the DECISIONS sense — it applies decisions 5 and 24 and OPERATING_MODEL §6, "the gate's `owner_actions` check re-judges the recorded actor", to the one place the session can write them). It already performs the owner's un-park labels before the session (`state/unpark.py`, the actor read from the PR's timeline) and trusts the session's `status.yaml` after it. From session 10 (plugin 0.2.26), after the session and before the hand-over, it compares the owner-only fields of the work branch's `status.yaml` with what the runner itself knows, and parks on a mismatch (`sdlc:needs-human`, "what I need from you" naming the field, the session's value and the expected one): (i) `risk_accepted_by` and `tests_unlocked_by` entries, `iterations_reset_by` and its stamp — each must be one the runner recorded before the session from a label event whose actor it read from GitHub, or already present on the base branch's copy; a new one the session wrote parks; (ii) `profile_override` and `review_override` — read from the default branch's `status.yaml` (the copy the owner approved at gate (a) or later), the branch's own value ignored and a change of it reported; (iii) `panel_calls` and `iterations` — may not drop below the runner's pre-session value unless the runner's own `sdlc:reset-iterations` handling lowered them. By hand (no runner) the gate's commit-author rules stay the check, as today. Rejected: (b), "the owner's merge at (e) is enough" — it leaves the Full profile's (c)/(d) label gates and the panel cap on the model's word, and OPERATING_MODEL §6 already promises the re-judging.
102. **The ruleset is checked where the owner reads daily, not at the gate**: the digest carries the notice (the workflow token's `contents: read` is enough; no phase run is blocked by a missing ruleset, since the owner's merge still stands between the run and `main`), and `/sdlc-init` §6 prescribes it in the owner's own settings.
