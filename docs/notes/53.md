## 53. The sample's default branch has a ruleset that restricts updates (ruleset 24078926)

### rulesets
- **The sample's `main` is covered by an active branch ruleset** (verified, the `rules` and `ruleset` reads): ruleset 24078926, named "main — owner merges only (decision 4)", `target: branch`, `enforcement: active`, its condition `ref_name.include: ["~DEFAULT_BRANCH"]`, created 2026-09-27 at 17:24 UTC and not updated since. Its four rules are `deletion`, `non_fast_forward`, `update` and `pull_request` (no required approving review, all three merge methods allowed).
