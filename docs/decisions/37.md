## 37. One profile and one review mode, `standard` and `deferred`, with no per-change override (amends 18, 21, 27, 30, 32, 34 and 35)

*Reference: steps 9a, 16a · NEW (2026-10-20), from ROADMAP R60's list of every optional feature (`docs/reviews/2026-10-20-lifecycle-axis-ports/options.md`, section 1, rows "Profile" and "Review mode", and the summary's note C3, which lists the decisions each mode touches)*

- Today, the profile (decision 18, as amended by 21 and 30): `sdlc.yaml: profile` is `standard` or `full`, and `status.yaml: profile_override` changes it for one change (`plugin/state/conventions.py`, `PROFILES` and `HUMAN_GATES`). `standard` waits for the owner at (a), (b) and (e), shaped by decision 30's fast lane. `full` also waits for the owner's `sdlc:c-approved` and `sdlc:d-approved` labels on the build PR after (c) and (d) (decisions 3 and 27, the owner's account only by decision 34; the runner's guard, `plugin/ci/run_phase.py:1968`). Under `full` the fast lane does not apply (`docs/OPERATING_MODEL.md` §4, "The fast lane"), and decision 35 also shows a risk-list hit in the banner of the PR that waits for those labels.
- Today, the review mode (decision 21, as amended by 32 and 35): `sdlc.yaml: review` is `deferred` or `parked`, and `status.yaml: review_override` changes it for one change. `deferred` puts a judgment item of decision 21's fixed list to the review panel and the run continues; `parked` parks the change for the owner. `/sdlc-init` proposes `deferred` (decision 32); a `sdlc.yaml` without the key still reads as `parked` (`conventions.py`, `DEFAULT_REVIEW_MODE`).
- In use: neither mode is. This repository's and the sample's `sdlc.yaml` both say `profile: standard` and `review: deferred`; no `status.yaml` under either repository's `changes/` sets a `profile_override`, and the one `review_override` set says `deferred` (verified, `grep` on 2026-10-20).
- **Accepted:**
  1. **One profile, `standard`.** The `full` profile is withdrawn, as decision 21 withdrew Lite. In every project gates (c) and (d) are passed by the confidence gate and park only on failure. The human gates are (a), (b) and (e) as decision 30 shapes them, and (f) as decision 25 has it. The `sdlc:c-approved` and `sdlc:d-approved` labels go with the profile.
  2. **One review mode, `deferred`.** The `parked` mode is withdrawn. A judgment item of decision 21's fixed list always goes to the review panel, the run continues, and the owner reads "Decisions taken for you" at the next human gate and overturns any of them with a review comment. What never goes to the panel and still parks is unchanged: decision 21's list as decision 35 amended it (an escalation on a risk item, a guardrail file change, a run limit, an infrastructure failure, a merge, a deploy).
  3. **No per-change override.** `status.yaml: profile_override` and `review_override` go, and `sdlc.yaml: profile` and `review` stop being settings. A risky change is held by the risk list and the close-look banner (decision 35) and, on a declared production, by the release label (decision 13 as amended by 36), not by a profile.
  4. **The code follows in a plugin version of its own,** after this file: the two modes and the overrides removed from `plugin/` and `template/`, with the documents that describe them (`docs/OPERATING_MODEL.md` §3 and §4, `template/sdlc.yaml`, the phase commands, `/sdlc-init`'s `--profile` and `--review`). It goes on the port list in PROGRESS if it touches `plugin/ci/run_phase.py` or the other listed paths. The root's and the sample's `sdlc.yaml` lines are the owner's edits (guardrail files), given in the owner's format by that build's session.
- Rejected: keeping both modes as choices (each doubles the combinations to try, and no project uses the second value); keeping `full` for projects with a live production or money at stake, decision 18's reason for it (the risk list, the banner and the release label hold those changes, and `full` adds two waits to every change); keeping the per-change overrides alone (a second place to set what no longer varies).

**Left to the build's design, not decided here:** how a file that still says `profile: full`, `review: parked` or carries an override is read, and whether it is reported once (the precedent: `LEGACY_PROFILES` reads an old `lite` as `standard` since plugin 0.2.14, `conventions.py`); and that a `sdlc.yaml` without `review:` then reads as `deferred`, where decision 32 kept it `parked` "so a project that never wrote it does not switch silently" (`conventions.py`, the review mode comment).

**Why:** The owner's concern of 2026-10-20, which the list was made for: every optional feature multiplies the combinations to try, and an option nobody turns on is work that never gets used (`options.md`, "Why this list"). `full` adds two waits per change against the owner's autonomy weight, while a risky change already waits through the risk list and the banner (row "Profile"); `parked` stops at every judgment call, and since decision 32 nobody uses it (row "Review mode"). The article's end state is the same mechanism for every project: "an independent confidence gate between stages, a deterministic check or an adversarial reviewing agent, deciding whether the previous stage's output continues or is escalated to a human" (p.42).

**What it gives up:** a project with money at stake can no longer ask for the owner's look after (c) and after (d), the reason decision 18 kept Full; that look comes at (e), with the banner, and on a declared production the release label stays the second act. The owner never decides a judgment item before a run goes on: the panel decides and the owner overturns afterwards, and every project pays the panel's runs, within `gate.max_budget_usd` and `gate.max_panel_calls`.

**What it amends:**
- decision 18: the Full profile is withdrawn and the per-change override removed; Standard, as decision 30 shapes it, is the only profile;
- decision 21: the `parked` mode and the per-change review override are removed, and "Standard: a, b, e; Full: all five" reads Standard alone; the panel, its fixed list and the parks that never go to it (as decision 35 amended them) are unchanged;
- decision 27: the (c) and (d) labels go with the Full profile, so no gate is approved by a label; decision 3, which 27 amends, is reached through it;
- decision 30: "the Full profile's (c) and (d) are unchanged" has nothing left to keep, and the fast lane applies to every project;
- decision 32: "`parked` stays available per project and per change" is withdrawn; the raised build-run limits stay;
- decision 34: point 3's approval of gates (c) and (d) with their labels goes; only the owner's account still starts a fix round;
- decision 35: point 1's banner on the PR that waits for `sdlc:c-approved` or `sdlc:d-approved` goes; the banner at (b) and (e) and the merge as the acceptance stay.

Kept: 11 (the hard parks), 13 as amended by 36 (gate (e) and the release label), 25 (gate (f)), 28 and 29.
