## 28. The owner accepts the Important review findings of a head with one label (amends 24)

*Reference: steps 19, 26 · NEW (2026-10-13), from the owner's answer of 2026-10-08 (ROADMAP R6, `docs/proposals/accept-verb.md`); proposed, for the owner's review together with decision 29*

- No new verb (what the framework does today): an Important finding at gate (e) is answered by a "Request changes" review that starts a fix round (decision 22), or, under `review: deferred`, by the review panel (decision 21).
- A comment verb: the owner waives one finding at a time in a review comment, which the next run parses. This is the "label or a comment" of the question carried since session 4; decision 24 moved the owner's un-park verbs from text to labels because GitHub records a label's actor.
- A label, `sdlc:accept-findings`, beside decision 24's three un-park labels: applied on a build PR, it accepts every Important finding recorded in `evidence/review-findings.json` for the head it was applied on, by `signature`, and nothing newer; the next run reads it and removes it like the other un-park labels, writes each accepted signature to `status.yaml` with the label's actor and the time, and lists the accepted findings under their own heading in the PR body; `check_findings` treats an accepted signature as not blocking and says so in its reason; a new Important finding with another signature still parks. **[RECOMMENDED]**

**To settle with the alternative (the proposal's "To settle in the decision and the design"):**

| Question | Answers | Recommended, and why |
|---|---|---|
| Where the label is honoured | Gate (e) only; also inside fix rounds at (c)–(e) | Gate (e) only: it is the proposal's "safer first version", and gate (e) is where the `findings` check requires the review pass (`plugin/gate/artifacts.py`, `FINDINGS_REQUIRED_AT`) |
| Whether an accepted finding still yields its `CLAUDE.md` proposal (`claude_md_first_time_lines`) | Yes, kept; no, suppressed | Kept: the proposal records a pattern, the waiver records one decision (the proposal's suggested answer) |
| The label's name | `sdlc:accept-findings`; another | `sdlc:accept-findings`, matching `sdlc:accept-risk` |

**Accepted:** pending the owner's review

**Why the recommendation:** At gate (e) the `findings` check parks while any Important finding stands (`plugin/gate/checks.py`, `check_findings`), and under `review: parked`, this repository's setting, a finding the owner judges harmless still costs a round. On 2026-10-08 four earlier nits graded Important parked change 0003 on the iteration cap; clearing it took a reset label, a five-item review and two fix rounds, about three hours and 14 USD, for four findings the owner would have waived in one step (`docs/proposals/accept-verb.md`, "The problem"). A label carries its actor, which is decision 24's reason for labels and what the `owner_actions` check reads, so the waiver is recorded with who gave it, as the article asks of an acceptance: "logged along with who accepted it" (p.17), approvals in the PR history (p.35). One label per head, bound to that head's findings by `signature`, cannot accept a finding the owner has not seen; the signatures already exist and are the key `changes/.review-seen.json` counts repeats by. A comment verb would bring back the text parsing decision 24 removed. The panel under `review: deferred` is untouched.

**Order:** built after the label race is fixed by the 0.3.8 runs PR (choice 180), so R6's design reads the fixed un-park code (ROADMAP R6, the session-24 annotation); in the owner's order, after R7 and R5. It changes `plugin/`, so it ships as a plugin version with tests.
