## 32. Deferred review is the default (amends 21)

*Reference: steps 12, 21 · NEW (2026-10-16), from the owner's question of 2026-10-15 on the pipeline's waits; ROADMAP R48, `docs/proposals/pipeline-speed.md`.*

- Today (decision 21): `/sdlc-init` proposes `review: parked`, so a judgment item inside a phase parks the change for the owner.
- **Accepted:** `review: deferred` is the default `/sdlc-init` proposes and the template carries: the review panel settles a judgment item in fresh contexts and the run continues; the owner reads "Decisions taken for you" at the next gate and overturns any of them with a review comment. `parked` stays available per project and per change. What parks in both modes is unchanged (a risk-list hit, a guardrail change, a run limit, an infrastructure failure, a merge, a deploy).
- With it, the run limits are raised for the build run (the template's turn and wall-clock caps), within the per-change budget cap, so a large build is not stopped and re-run a day later (change 0003 hit the 200-turn cap).

**What it gives up:** the owner decides judgment items after the fact instead of before; the panel's runs cost tokens; a higher worst-case spend per run.

**What it amends:** decision 21 (the default mode, not the mechanism).

**Accepted:** the owner, 2026-10-16 ("Approved", on the table of 2026-10-15, rows 4 and 7).
