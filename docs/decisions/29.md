## 29. In CI a red target starts the next fix round by itself (amends 11 and PROGRESS choice 154)

*Reference: steps 16, 19, 28, 30 · NEW (2026-10-13), from study entry T10 (crosswalk rows test-loop and test-verify), brought forward as ROADMAP R10 (`docs/proposals/test-loop-closes-in-ci.md`); written with decision 28 for one owner review: the mechanism accepted on 2026-10-13 (PR #168), two of its three settle questions revised the same day after the owner's caveat and pending the owner's word*

- A red target found by the runner parks the change, and the owner's act is the round (what the framework does today, choice 154 of 0.3.2): a "Request changes" review (decision 22), a `workflow_dispatch` of `sdlc-fix.yml` or an un-park label starts the next fix round; the article's run, fix and re-run loop needs the owner between rounds.
- The runner dispatches the next fix round itself: when its commands step finds a red target, it dispatches a fix round explicitly (events caused by the workflow token start no run that proceeds by itself), with the failing logs as the round's constraint; the iteration count goes up by one, the runner runs the targets again after the round, and the change parks only at the iteration cap (`gate.max_iterations`, 3, or `max_iterations_non_routine`, 2) with its partial evidence, as any limit does (decision 11). In CI the verifier's report names the record it read, by phase and commit, and says that the final tree's result is the runner's, shown in the check run `sdlc/d`. The runner's single run of build, test and lint after the session, outside the sandbox, stays. **[ACCEPTED]** (the owner, 2026-10-13, PR #168)
- The targets run inside the sandbox again, so the session sees a red target itself: rejected in choices 133 and 154, since the sandbox denies a nested `.env` to every process the session starts, and renaming fixtures or narrowing the deny rule was not chosen.
- A helper that runs the targets for the session outside the sandbox while it works: a hole in the sandbox that the session controls.

**To settle with the alternative (target-design T10, "Open").** The first recommendations of 2026-10-13 cost the owner attention and autonomy in two rows (one counter: automatic rounds could use up the cap before the owner's review; parking at once: those cases wait for the owner). The owner's caveat the same day: neither criterion may go down. The two rows were revised with T30's rule, the owner's word of 2026-10-09 ("the manager's one reset at the cap", `target-design.md` T30 and T1), and one re-run; they wait for the owner's word.

| Question | Answers | Recommended, and why |

**Accepted:** the mechanism (2) and the third question, by the owner on 2026-10-13 (PR #168); the first two questions pending the owner's word on the revised recommendations

**Why the recommendation:** The article's loop is run, fix and re-run until the check passes, "so what reaches the engineer has already passed it" (p.27). Since plugin 0.3.2 no project target runs inside the CI session: the runner runs them once after the session, outside the sandbox, and parks the change on a red target (`plugin/gate/gate.py`, `run_deferred_commands`), so each round waits for the owner (choice 154, `docs/progress/session-18.md`). The owner chose the blend on the crosswalk row test-loop on 2026-10-03: keep the runner's single run, and have a red target start the next round by itself, so that what reaches the owner has passed the check or used up its rounds; and on the row test-verify: keep the split, and have the verifier say which tree it read, so nobody reading `verifier.md` later takes the earlier tree's results for the final ones (`docs/reviews/2026-09-28-playbook-vs-framework/target-design.md`, T10). The two sandbox alternatives were considered and not chosen there. Decision 11's "park, never page" stands: a limit hit still parks and nobody is notified; only the park on the first red target goes.

**What it amends:** decision 11 (as amended by 21), since a red target found by the runner no longer parks at once; PROGRESS choice 154 (`docs/progress/session-18.md`), whose "the owner's `/sdlc-fix` is the round" no longer holds in CI. Choice 154's split of the run (the runner runs the targets, the verifier reads) stays.

**Order:** accepted by the owner on 2026-10-09 "to run before 1.0.0", after R6 and before the carried gaps (ROADMAP R10). Since then 1.0.0 has become the item-11 PR alone, and the session-26 handoff's order (`docs/handoffs/sessions/2026-10-13-session-26.md`, step 4; in `HANDOFF.md` until PR #170) puts R7, R5, R6 and R10 after that read; the two sources disagree on whether R10 precedes 1.0.0 (an open question for the owner). It changes `plugin/` and the fix workflow, so it ships as a plugin version with tests.
