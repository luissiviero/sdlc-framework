## 31. A pull request that waits for the owner requests the owner's review (amends 11 and 20)

*Reference: steps 12, 13 · NEW (2026-10-16), from the owner's question of 2026-10-15 on the pipeline's waits; ROADMAP R47, `docs/proposals/pipeline-speed.md`.*

- Today (decisions 11 and 20): a park writes "what I need from you" and notifies no one; the owner finds waiting work through the daily digest or by looking, once or twice a day.
- **Accepted:** when a pull request starts waiting for the owner (a human gate, or a park), the automation requests the owner's review on it, so GitHub's own notification reaches the owner (the mobile app's push, which the owner can mute or filter); nothing else notifies, and the park, its label and "what I need from you" stay as they are.
- Rejected: a notification channel of the framework's own (mail, chat): a second channel to maintain; keeping no notification (the owner's reaction time per gate, about 4 to 12 hours).

**What it gives up:** a few interruptions a day, at the moments the owner's act is the next step.

**What it amends:** decision 11 ("never a notification": a review request at a waiting gate is the one exception) and decision 20 (the digest stays, no longer the only way finished work reaches the owner). The line in `CLAUDE.md` ("Escalation means park … never a notification") is the owner's edit, given as a table in the session file.

**Accepted:** the owner, 2026-10-16 ("Approved", on the table of 2026-10-15, row 2).
